=============================================================================
All numbers below are from arbs_dd_unit_v1 / arbs_usd_swap_tape_legs_v3 /
arbs_dd_curve_mid_v1 / arbs_dd_unit_bucket_v1 on the PRODUCTION Supabase,
read-only, pulled by scratch/wfstr_*.py in this worktree.

Sample: 138 as_of_dates, 2024-07-01 .. 2026-06-15, in 6 blocks spanning the
whole 26-month window (322,755 leg rows, 237,995 units).  The table carries a
SINGLE code_vintage (2939a2760e5c) over all 1,283,522 rows, so there is no
vintage drift for a subsample to miss.

VERDICT: NOT REFUTED for the arithmetic, the leg order, the quote weights and
the units.  ONE material correction: the formula holds only under
rule = 'RATE_VS_MID'.

   backfill_dealer_direction.py writes deviation_bps = tp - mp ONLY under

       CURVE   n=317  median|err| 0.431 bp  p95 21.99  max 141.95  0 exact
       FLY     n=110  median|err| 0.361 bp  max 119.56            0 exact
       OUTRIGHT n=888 median|err| 1.192 bp  max 2.68e7            0 exact
   Population: 82,004 CURVE units (46.7% of all CURVE) and 36,218 FLY (41.6%)
   carry NPV_VS_UPFRONT.  The claim's own nTested reproduces the rate-rule
   grid-clean subset exactly (4,629 CURVE / 2,129 FLY over 138 days scales to
   ~17.7k / ~8.1k over 527 days vs the claimed 17,197 / 8,495), so the filter
   existed in their work but is absent from the stated formula.

       canonical  494 / 494 exact, max 1.279e-13 bp
       reversed     0 / 494,       median 1.600 bp, max 200.0 bp
   195 of the 494 are grid-UNREACHABLE, i.e. outside the claim's tested set.
