== SIGN ==
Stored vector is dNPV/d(+1bp zero bump) of the DEALER-SIGNED position. To land the
pinned convention (+ = dealer RECEIVED = long duration = delta_dv01 > 0) it needs the
same single -1.0 flip as ladder.py's RL_DELTA_TO_FUTURES_EQ. One constant, all buckets.

== FAILURE MODES ==
1. past-effective legs (339,497 = 14.6%): rateslib RAISES
   "effective date for rate period is before the initial node date of the Curve...
    neglected to supply appropriate fixings". Hits BOTH methods equally.
   Analytic fix: comp factor over [s,e] = R_realised * DF(today)/DF(e); only DF(e), DF(p)
   carry curve sensitivity. Needs a published SOFR/EFFR fixings series.
2. maturity beyond the last bucket: total conserved, placement wrong. A 50Y trade on a
   30Y-capped grid puts 157,374 of 234,097 USD/bp into the 30Y bucket; with 40Y/50Y
   pillars it splits 96,326 / 34,387 / 26,661 across 50Y/40Y/30Y.
3. tent time measure: daily_dcf is 1/360 on these Act360 curves, not 1/365. Hard-coding
   1/365 scales every bucket by 1.39% uniformly (cancels in the par conversion).
4. USD-FEDFUNDS-1D is NOT in RATESLIB_CURVE_DEFINITIONS -> reconstruct_curve warns and
   falls back to act360/nyc/mf (correct for USD, but unpinned).
5. single-curve assumption: telescoping needs forecast == discount. True for the SOFR and
   FED_FUNDS OIS curves here; a BASIS leg (15,259) or a dual-curve build breaks it.
