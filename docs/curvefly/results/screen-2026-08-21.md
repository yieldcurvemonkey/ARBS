# Curve-fly screen — 2026-08-21 (USD SOFR)

1,075 structures from 71 legs; 1,019 survive the degeneracy gate. Carry-and-roll
is static-curve, one year. `rac = carry / (realised daily vol x sqrt(252))`;
`rac_net` charges the position for full reversion to its 2-year mean. See
`../DESIGN.md`, especially section 6 — **the two columns disagree and the
disagreement is the point**.

The forward curve of a fixed tenor (`f1 x t / f2 x t`) is the carry family and
the short-convexity one. Forward flies are where convexity is sold to you: only
30% carry positively, averaging -2.55bp/yr. That is the linear long-vol vehicle
and that is its price.

## Caveats

* The screen ranks carry per unit of realised vol. It knows nothing about
  bid-offer: the top raw-`rac` name is `10y1y/20y1y` (33.8bp of carry) and a
  1y tail 10-20 years forward is nothing like as liquid as a 10y tail.
* Half-lives are estimated in-sample over two years; mean-reversion parameters
  are the least stable quantity here. Direction solid, magnitudes indicative.
* `rac_net` assumes the 2-year mean is the attractor. If the forward curve is
  structurally repricing rather than mean-reverting, `rac` is the better guide.
  That is a view, not a screen output — but the trade requires one.
* 47 structures were excluded for realising under 0.25bp/day: their legs sit
  between the same curve nodes, so the level is near-constant by construction.
  Before that gate they were the top AND bottom of the ranking.
