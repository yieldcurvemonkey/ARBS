# The framework: one risk, five wrappers, one ledger

Every report in the corpus is the same trade wearing different clothes: someone is long a payoff convex in rates, someone is short it, and the market charges a running rent for the convex side. The corpus contains **five linear wrappers of rates variance plus the option market itself**:

1. **Ultra-long forward curve** — DV01-neutral flatteners are straddles (convexity differential between tenors); the standing inversion of 30s/50s and 10y10y/20y10y **is** the option premium, set by LDI receiving.
2. **SOFR futures CA** — packs/bundles vs matched IMM swap; the future is linear and margined, the cleared swap convex and PAI-carrying; the CA is the financing-bias rent. (Huggins–Schaller nuance: SR3 has *no* ED-style payout convexity — settlement at the end of the reference quarter makes the futures rate a true forward; the entire SR3 CA is margining bias + positioning premium, which is what Ho-Lee prices.)
3. **Curve flies** — the discrete second derivative of the curve in maturity space: priced curvature. Paying the belly ≈ buying a straddle **only in the priced-rent sense** — see the constraint layer.
4. **UST futures basis** — net basis = the short's delivery option (switch + wildcard + EOM), an actual option floored at zero.
5. **Swap spreads vs vol** — convexity-hedger flow (MBS/banks/VA/Formosa) makes spreads conditionally directional.
6. **Swaptions / SOFR options / UST futures options** — the explicit venues, and the pricing source for the other five.

**The gamma-vs-theta measurement — four numbers per structure, everything in bp/day:**

- **Γ$** — by *repricing on shifted curves*, never closed forms (`GAMMA_01` raises on our backend; `Curve.translate` silently cancels on DV01-neutral packages). Convexity per unit DV01 grows ∝ maturity: the forward-space ladder per $1,000/bp PV01 runs 10y $1.1 → 10y10y $3.1 → 20y10y $5.1 → 40y10y $9.1 per bp². For the CA, the gamma is the matched swap's declining hedge ladder (CME's 2y chain: 100/99/…/95 = 779 lots; ±100bp P&L +$22.3k/+$21.2k ≈ (797−779)·½·100·$25).
- **θ$** — always decomposed: carry (funding-spread ≡ forward-minus-spot per unit DV01), rolldown (static-curve ageing; forwards-realized is the zero-edge null), and **decay of the convex object itself** (CA theta: dCA/dt = −σ²·mean(T1)/1e4, recovered by the quarterly roll jump at ratio 1.02–1.11; the basis's daily gross-basis drop = the CTD's carry). Forward-starting structures have zero carry — all rent is roll (Nordea's own cards print exactly this).
- **σ_BE = √(2·θ_daily/Γ)** — the universal axis. Γ cancels out of the hurdle: **size scales the P&L, never the breakeven**. JPM's "curve-implied vol" is this at a 1y horizon; Citi's "daily BE" is this truncated at zero carry (56% of 2023 truncated — trade the continuous ratio instead).
- **σ_impl / σ_rlzd** — invert the wrapper's model on its price (Ho-Lee `CA = ½σ²·mean(T1²)·1e4` for CA; the delivery-option model for basis) vs matched-window realized ex roll dates. The linking algebra: `dΠ = ½Γ(σ_i)S²[(dS/S)² − σ_i²dt]` exactly; `E[dΠ] = ½ΓS²(σ_r²−σ_i²)dt` on average; the vol-linear `ν(σ_r−σ_i)` only when σ_r≈σ_i — **variance, not vol** (at IV 2bp/day, RV 2.1 pays 10c but RV 2.2 pays 30c). Citi writes the identical identity for the CA seller: selling CA ≡ selling delta-hedged straddles.

**The two-sided franchise:** rank the book on σ_BE/σ_rlzd and flip at the published thresholds — **long gamma (delta-hedged flatteners) at ≤ 0.42–0.8, exit 0.8; short gamma (fwd steepeners, short CA) at ≥ 1.17–1.38**, harvested by the mechanical resize (recalc DV01 every 15–30bp, resize to neutral — each resize books ½Γ·threshold²; the PM's chat describes exactly this, and Citi's alerts executed it at 20–25bp).

# The venues, with the trades

**1 · Long end (JPM founding franchise + Citi engine).** JPM 2017: two signals — expected payoff of the aged flattener under the 1Yx30Y swaption-implied distribution, and curve-implied vol vs that swaption in bp/day. Backtest (daily entries post-2009, straddle-funded): 30s/50s 70% cheap-share, 56% hit, +10.4bp avg, **carry −100.6bp**; 25Y/20Yx5Y **100% cheap-share, 86% hit, +11.4bp, carry +1.1bp** — forwards give the same convexity without the carry bill. JPM 2018 globalizes it: E(R) = carry + slide + ½σ²Γ; gilt fit cvx = 0.49σ²−0.6σ+1.05 (R² 99%); **USD & GBP cheap, EUR mixed, JPY expensive; best structure 20Y/40Yx10Y**. Citi 2019 runs it systematically: $100k DV01 flatteners resized per 25bp, Sharpes 0.05–0.35, **P&L skew ≈ 0 vs −3 for the short-straddle benchmark** (Sharpe 0.84 at a 25%-delta threshold). The two full lifecycles are the replication anchors: USD 15y5y/20y10y entered 5/9/19 @ −11.8bp (BE/RV 0.42), closed 12/5/19 @ −12.5bp, **+$155k net on $50k DV01 with the curve nearly unchanged** (~$32k costs); GBP 15y10y/25y10y entered at *positive* carry (+0.5bp/y, "free convexity"), closed 26-Mar-20, **+£445k = £115k curve + £330k convexity**. The 12-Jun-23 piece runs the same book short: steepener screen with BE/RV 1.17–1.38 on 10y10y/15y15y and 10y10y/20y5y (carry 10.31bp/y at −1.47σ since-2000).

**2 · SOFR CA (Citi's screen, 2016–2023).** The ticket ledger: sell Blues 9-Feb-17 @ 8.8bp + fitted 2s5s10s fly hedge → 6.6bp, **+$500k net** ($200k DV01); Greens 6-Jun-17 @ 4.3 → 2.65, **+$475.5k net** (round-trip cost ~0.07bp); Blues 5-Jan-18 @ 6.5 (+$140k/3m carry); outright 18-Apr-18 @ 7.2; Blues 11-Jun-21 @ 7.9, target 4bp/stop 3bp, 5.9 in ten days; SOFR-era Blues 17-Feb-23 @ 12.7, target 4/stop 2.5, +1.5bp/3m carry. Entries always ~2σ VsModel with roll and implied/realized support; hedges = rolled regressions (2s5s10s fly `10.2+21.4·(−0.73,1,−0.47)` → refit three weeks later — the drift is the instruction), the ED6/ED16 strip proxy (`−0.65+0.044·(ED16−0.74·ED6)`, invalid at ZLB). Positioning is the conditioning variable: futures are the capital-efficient short (1–2 day IM vs 5-day), so **CA above model = short base, at/below = longs**; Δ(CA−model) on Δdealer positioning R² 0.32. JPM's 2017 version reads CA net of theory as an *implied funding spread* (L+50–100 → L+300) peaking at Greens, and pairs long-CA-richness with long front-end CME-LCH basis. CCP layer: basis <0.1bp (2014) → 2bp (May-2015) → 3.4bp 30y (2017, fully explained as two-sided MVA) → ~0.85bp with a 2024 sign flip; **trade floor ~3.9–4bp**; breaks at 13-May-15, Oct-2020 discounting, Sept-24 FMX-at-LCH.

**3 · Flies.** Citi 2010 "Flying Too High" is the template ticket: receive belly 1y-fwd 5s10s30s @ 48bp (98th pctile) vs a regime-probability fair value of 19, **target = 28bp mispricing − 8bp convexity rent at 120bp/y vol ≈ 20, stop 10** — every fly trade should carry that rent line. JPM's EUR beta-stability framework is the best-tested entry machinery in the corpus: 6M two-factor residual, enter R²≥60% & |z|≥1.5 & |resid|≥4bp, exit zero-cross/+2SD/1M horizon, **skip when the beta-stability index √(Zb²+Zw²) > 3** — 1.3bp/trade, 52% hit, 1,188 trades 2001–21, failure regimes named (cycle turns). Citi's Jun-2020 1x2x1 (receive 20y10y vs 10y10y & 30y10y, entered 16bp off a z-vs-vol-adjusted-carry frontier, +$360k) prints the operational lesson: package b/o ~3bp, untradeable at the Mar-2020 max-P&L.

**4 · Basis (the richest venue in the corpus).** Burghardt–Belton: `gross = net + carry`; **BNOC = the market price of the delivery options**; IRR < term repo always, the gap is the premium; OAB = actual − (carry + theoretical option value); "the basis is rich if OAB > 0, and if the basis is rich, futures are cheap." The worked straddle: buy the CTD basis at BNOC 4.53/32 — unchanged lose ~3, −60bp +7, +60bp +19.5. **Ch.7 is the direct ancestor of our basis-vs-vol program**: input options-market vol into the delivery model, trade OAB against the matched strip of real options — 297-trade simulation at |OAB|>3: **+4.9 ticks avg, σ 8.6, vs +6.8/σ 13.4 unhedged** (the option leg is variance reduction), with the caution that options on futures expire ~1 month before the futures so **the basis leg carries more vega than the real options**. Yield betas move the crossover with the market "as if the strike were raised" — the mechanism that killed basis gamma 1991–93 and the regime variable to watch. Citi Summer lull executes the cross-venue comparison: **$100mm May37 basis = $420/bp² of gamma for $130k vs $187k for the same gamma in a 1m25y swaption** (±40bp risk/reward 1.56 vs 1.3). JPM's series adds the machinery: implied repo and BNOC are the same information but implied repo blows up into the roll (WN: 50–100bp quarterly implied-vs-term gaps "from a few ticks of option value"); the wildcard priced by backward induction at 1–1.5bp/2hr post-close vol (2bp FOMC) — **FVM8 optimally delivered late even at −3.5 ticks of gross basis; CF < 0.9 wait, CF ≈ 0.95 deliver**; Mar-2020 makes implied repo the system barometer. BofA Apr-2025 is the current entry: TY/UXY nets 0 to −0.5 ticks = the whole delivery month of optionality at ≤0 premium; buy T 4.375 01/32 vs TYM5 at net 0, **target +1 tick, stop −4**. RiskVal supplies the desk spec: distance-to-switch in σ = the printed moneyness; option-adjusted net basis = NB − Σoptions·CF; calendar-roll fair value off a smooth forward ASW-Z curve.

**5 · The option panel (Citi Vol Lab).** Fifteen standing modules — macro-vol fair value (ATM on 3 curve PCs, ex-ZLB), implied/realized incl. intraday and by-time-of-day, forward-vol efficient frontier (gamma-neutral calendars ranked by vol-adjusted roll), **skew vs an empirical SABR calibrated to realized dynamics** (the cleanest published realized-skew recipe), skew-as-positioning (RR percentile → next-1m rates: <25th ⇒ −11.5bp, >75th ⇒ +8.8bp), **board vs CTD-matched swaptions** (the basis triangle's third leg), zero-cost conditionals, CMS curve vol/implied correlation, Formosa vega-supply. The short-gamma benchmark every wrapper must beat: 1m10y delta-hedged straddle selling, Sharpe 0.84 with a 25%-delta threshold, 1997–2017.

**6 · Bassman + Nordea.** Bassman gives the laws in dollar form: the **3:1 rule** (buy the option when roll-down ≥ ~3× premium — "a 3-to-1 payoff for a 2-to-1 risk"; 1y2y @ 56bp vs 82bp roll, 2.88×; 1y1y @ 47bp vs 132bp unhedged); **the Positive Carry Hedge anomaly** (10y-expiry payer whose constant-OTM price *rises* with expiry when the forwards and the vol surface are both inverted — negative duration + positive convexity + **positive carry**; the 2008 vintage more than doubled with spot lower — the generalizable scan: walk constant-moneyness option prices along the expiry axis); spread vol = √(σ_A²+σ_B²−2ρσ_Aσ_B) — curve options as the most levered vol buy at peak correlation; **"a high OAS when IVol is low only means you are selling IVol less cheaply"**; the flow equivalences ($100mm new MBS ≈ $50mm 3y10y straddles of homeowner vega; the Fed's $1.25T ≈ selling $625bn straddles); the FVA as pure vega (spot 100nv purchasable 6m forward at 95nv); "carry is theta in dollars" ($100mm 2s10s steepener ≈ $390k/month to hold). Nordea supplies the screener culture: carry vs roll pinned (their printed formula omits the accrual τ — 2× off if transcribed; forward-starts print carry 0.0 on their own cards), the **J-score** (convexity-adjusted carry/vol + z), the **convexity grid** (spot 1y 0.3bp → 30y-fwd 1y **13.1bp — the forward-start dimension dominates tenor**), the vol↔curvature PCA bridge (their R 5s10s30s "steepener" fly earned +3bp from 10s30s and **+15bp from the curvature drop** — a short-vol trade in curve clothing), and the **"brain-dead trade"** — receive 10y5y/pay 15y5y at the top of the forward curve, rolls peaking +13.5bp/y at 6–7y forward — the EUR twin of our own finding that same-tenor forward curves are the harvest family (+6.63bp/y mean CR, 78% positive, across our 1,075-structure screen).

# The constraint layer (what our own backtests forbid)

- **A fly is not a vol hedge.** Level R² up to 0.88 on 10y10y/20y10y vs 10Yx10Y nvol (β −1.13 — the desk intuition is correctly *signed*), but partial R² after level/slope controls 0.000–0.044 on all 11 legs, weekly change R² 0.0003–0.089, sign-flipping β. Flies are curvature/carry instruments *priced off* vol. CA-vs-fly pairing is dead four ways (515- and 298-cell pre-registered grids; "no hedge beats every hedge").
- **The CA roll jump is its theta** — a roll blackout leaves +1.2bp/quarter of one-sided short-CA carry that must be decomposed out of any Sharpe; blackouts must union both IMM clocks (22 dates each, zero shared); t+1 fills (same-day fills took hit rates 0.93 → 0.45).
- **Panels are not P&L**: Δrate×DV01 overstated hedged CA books 1.5–6× in Sharpe; engine-certify at ≥0.99 daily-change correlation.
- **Hedge ratios at the holding horizon** (β 0.61→1.22 from 1d→15d on tick-quantised marks; near 1, use flat 1:1), regression-tested through the origin (the lattice ratios were 5.7× too large for a density payoff — hedge a density with a density), restated as σ moves (variance-linear, not vol-linear).
- **DV01-neutral is not factor-neutral**: humped PC1 leaves 3.8–16.7% level leakage; the slope residual varies 130× across structures under the same rule — PC1-hedge the tight pairs, never book 5s30s-type pairs as convexity. The tight forward pairs survive PC1-neutral sizing (convexity 55–172% of P&L, t 2.7–5.4); 5s/30s dies (slope 259.6% at t 39.5).
- **Support gate, cost gate, null gate**: positions priced outside the quoted vol grid manufactured our strongest fake result (+1.94 → −0.19 on support); costs per leg at 0×/0.5×/1×/2× (anchors: swaps 0.3bp, flattener 0.75–1bp entry + 0.3–0.4 per resize, fly packages ~3bp, CCP basis floor ~4bp); deflated Sharpe at n_eff on both clocks with negative controls that must fail.
- **Everything in bp/day** — bp/yr vs bp/day is a silent √252 that correlation checks can't catch; guard 0.5–40bp/day.

Two split verdicts worth stating plainly: **the JPM long-end signal is degenerate as a timing rule** (the curve has been the cheaper gamma on 97%+ of days since 2015 — treat it as a structural allocation, with 5s/30s the only two-sided pair and that one a slope trade); and **the PM's strikeless-vol trade is right in form, wrong in timing** — breakeven daily move 5.81bp vs 1.43 realized (4.05×) beats the same-carry short straddle (zero vega vs $11,407/bp), but the entry sits at the 90th percentile with a 3.1-month AR(1) half-life (−3.4bp net at 4.5m unless the inversion unwind is structural), `10y10y/15y10y` is 60% better on risk-adjusted carry, and the fly leg hedges nothing.
