## The verdict

**The defensible per-leg ATM half-spread line for backtests, in annual normal bp
(measured as median |print IV − same-day EOD cube mid| on near-close ATM straddles,
2Y/5Y/10Y tails pooled):**

| expiry | half-spread (annual bp) | ÷15.87 (bp/day) | IQR (bp) | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1M | **0.71** | 0.045 | 0.29–1.47 | 580 |
| 2–3M | **0.38** | 0.024 | 0.16–0.89 | 743 |
| 6M | **0.25** | 0.016 | 0.09–0.58 | 497 |
| 9M–1Y | **0.21** | 0.013 | 0.07–0.51 | 803 |
| 18M–2Y | **0.23** | 0.014 | 0.09–0.57 | 539 |
| 3–5Y | **0.17** | 0.010 | 0.07–0.41 | 616 |

These are **upper bounds on the half-spread** (the mid is EOD, the print is intraday, and
the diff also absorbs any surface-marking difference between Citi's EOD and the executing
dealer), but the smear has been driven down two ways at once: straddles are first-order
insensitive to intraday forward drift, and the near-close (<2h to 5pm ET) subset minimizes
vol drift. The signed medians are −0.05 to −0.14 bp (prints fractionally *below* Citi's
mid), so the |diff| is not hiding a level bias. The line is stable across years (near-close
ATM straddle median 0.35 / 0.25 / 0.27 bp in 2024 / 2025 / 2026) and consistent with the
BofA-2024-primer anchor (~1.9 bp package cost on a 3-leg 300m package ⇒ per-leg ATM
half-spread plausibly 0.3–1.0 bp). Use ~**0.2–0.4 bp annual (≈0.015–0.025 bp/day) for
liquid 3M–1Y × 2Y–10Y ATM cells, ~0.7 bp at 1M expiry**, and treat outright (delta-unhedged)
fills as materially worse in practice (below).

Side (buy vs sell) is **not** inferable from the SDR public tape — no aggressor flag — so
this is an unconditional half-spread proxy, not a signed effective spread.

## What was measured vs what was assumed

**Measured, with an in-data discriminator:**

1. **Premium timing is FORWARD premium** (premium paid at the swap-start of expiry, not
   upfront). Under the spot hypothesis the ATM-straddle signed diff grows with expiry
   exactly like the funding leg; under forward it is flat (runner table):

   | expiry | n | fwd hyp (bp) | spot hyp (bp) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | <3M | 7,458 | −0.34 | +0.18 |
   | 3–9M | 6,427 | −0.13 | +1.47 |
   | 9–18M | 6,964 | −0.12 | +3.82 |
   | 18M–3Y | 6,008 | −0.17 | +7.76 |
   | >3Y | 5,922 | −0.11 | +23.86 |

   All headline numbers use the forward-premium reading (both IV columns are stored).

2. **UPI-vintage straddle pairs duplicate the package total onto both legs.** 35,217 of
   35,605 detected payer+receiver pairs carry byte-equal premiums on both legs; 3,232 of
   them are struck ≥5bp off-forward where equal *true* leg premiums are impossible
   (P_call − P_put = N·A·(F−K)). Read as total: ATM straddle median diff −0.15 bp; read as
   per-leg: +88 bp (nonsense). The 134 pre-UPI single-row STRADDLE prints (premium
   unambiguous) agree with the "total" reading (−0.37 bp).

3. **Pre-UPI date semantics are reporter-inconsistent**: 'Expiration Date' is sometimes the
   option expiry and sometimes the underlying swap end. Resolved as: option expiry = 'First
   exercise date' (fallback 'Expiration Date'); swap end = 'Maturity date of the underlier'
   if > expiry+60d, else 'Expiration Date' if > expiry+60d. This recovered ~3,100 rows per
   30 files that a naive reading discarded.

4. **FxdFlt-vs-OIS pooling**: USD swaptions appear under two UPI FISN families
   (`...OIS USD` and `...Fxd Flt USD`, both annual/ACT-360 SOFR underliers). A computed
   gate (per-cell ATM medians must agree within the OIS IQR) passed in **all 47 cells**
   with n≥10 on both sides → pooled.

**Assumed (stated, not verified in-data):** standard USD SOFR OIS underlying conventions
(annual fixed ACT/360, MF on NYC calendar, 2-day payment lag, swap start = expiry + 2bd) —
though verification (iii) pins these to rateslib's own swaption machinery at machine
precision; single-curve OIS discounting; ACT/365F expiry time from the cube date; cube
interpolation linear in strike offset and bilinear in log-expiry-years / log-tenor-years
(flat outside the grid, out-of-grid prints flagged and excluded from cells).

## The smear, quantified (why the straddle near-close number is the estimate)

Prints are intraday; the mid is 5pm ET. An outright's IV inherits intraday **forward**
drift at dσ/dF ≈ Φ(d)/vega ≈ 4.3 per unit at 1M expiry — so outright |diff| is dominated
by drift, not spread. Two independent handles agree (runner tables, ATM |off|<25bp):

| hours to close | outright med \|diff\| (n) | straddle med \|diff\| (n) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1h | 1.70 (1,634) | 0.29 (3,651) |
| 1–2h | 1.90 (1,627) | 0.30 (2,930) |
| 2–4h | 2.68 (3,364) | 0.37 (6,858) |
| 4–8h | 5.95 (11,803) | 0.65 (15,669) |
| 8–24h | 8.04 (1,155) | 0.74 (3,669) |

A real spread is time-invariant; both universes instead grow with distance from the close —
outrights by ~4.7x, straddles by ~2.5x — so the all-hours headline is smear-dominated and
the near-close numbers are the better estimates. Even at 0–1h the outright number (1.70 bp)
sits ~6x above the straddle number (0.29 bp): residual forward drift over the last hour plus
the fact that outright risk transfer genuinely prices wider than premium-matching straddles.
The premium-space cross-check agrees: near-close ATM straddle median |premium diff| is
1.07 bp of notional ≈ 0.30 bp of vol after vega conversion — same answer from price space.

**Headline upper-bound tables** (all-hours, outright, non-package, non-capped;
`swaption_cost_summary.txt` has the full grid): ATM 1M–1Y × 2Y/5Y/10Y runs 1.8–7.9 bp,
falling to 1.4–3.6 bp inside 2h of the close — read these as smear ceilings, not costs.

| expiry | outright <2h, 2Y | 5Y | 10Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1M | 6.33 (45) | 3.12 (104) | 2.58 (262) |
| 2–3M | 3.62 (38) | 3.51 (81) | 2.41 (258) |
| 6M | 2.11 (31) | 1.71 (38) | 1.52 (75) |
| 9M–1Y | 2.09 (84) | 1.51 (74) | 1.43 (222) |

## Caveats and quarantines

* **Upper bound, always**: even near-close numbers contain last-hour drift and any
  Citi-vs-street marking difference. Signed medians (−0.05..−0.14 bp) bound the latter.
* **Buy/sell side is not observable** in the CFTC public tape; per-print signed cost is
  therefore not identified. Payer-vs-receiver signed medians (−0.40 / +0.02 bp at ATM)
  show no material forward bias in the curve.
* **Quarantined universes measure wider, as expected** (ATM |diff|: package legs 5.69 bp,
  capped blocks 6.17 bp, late reports 12.88 bp) and are excluded from the headline but
  kept flagged in the parquet.
* **Inversion-failure selection**: ~8% of prints (mostly 5–50bp offsets) sit below
  intrinsic at the EOD forward and drop out, which flatters ITM cells slightly; ATM cells
  (1,477 of ~60k) are essentially unaffected.
* Deep-wing and small-n cells in `swaption_cost_cells.parquet` can be wild (tick-size
  premia, tiny vega, a few absurd notionals — 289 headline rows carry notional >$5bn from
  raw-tape typos); use cells with n ≥ 20 and |offset| ≤ 100bp.
* 5,973 pre-UPI strikes arrived percent-quoted under a decimal notation code and were
  rescued (÷100, flagged `strike_rescued`); excluding them moves the ATM outright median
  by 0.11 bp (4.60 → 4.48), i.e. nothing.
* The cube's own strike axis is offsets from **Citi's** forward; this study measures
  offsets from the same Citi curve the cube backends use, so the axes are consistent by
  construction, but both differ from any dealer's intraday forward — part of the smear.
