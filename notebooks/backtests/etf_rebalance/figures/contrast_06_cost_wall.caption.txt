Gross against net basis points per trade for the 1,818 grid configurations (of 5,192 run across the TLT league table and the wide TLT/TLH/IEF grids) that carry both figures. Each point's vertical distance below the y = x line — drawn as a stem for a sample of them — is its own measured round-trip cost on FedInvest's published bid and offer, and the colour is that cost. The cloud sits entirely below break-even: 2 rows show a positive net and they are the same single-trade cell listed twice, 0 configurations are net-positive on 50 trades or more, and none clears the deflated-Sharpe hurdle. The lower panel puts the gross and cost distributions on one common axis rather than a second scale, over the wider set of 4,779 cells that carry a gross: only 42 produce a gross larger than the 0.535bp median 20-30y butterfly round trip, and all 42 are TLH configurations whose own measured cost is 1.20-1.88bp. For context the entire cross-sectional richness dispersion this trade could ever capture is 0.434bp — 0.81x of ONE round trip.
