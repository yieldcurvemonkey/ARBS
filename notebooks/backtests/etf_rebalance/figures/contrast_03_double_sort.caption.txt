Mean forward richening by (richness quintile x active-weight tercile), TLT. In the heatmap the colour varies almost entirely along the richness axis: within a richness quintile, moving from the fund's most underweight bonds to its most overweight bonds changes the forward move by a few thousandths of a basis point. The two bar panels give the high-minus-low spread per quintile, for the 10-day book and for the 63-day bucket book with composition held fixed at entry. Both are non-monotone and sign-flipping across the five quintiles. A naive t of 4 that survives neither a HAC correction (figure 2) nor a double sort is a linear artefact. Source: _data/realized_double_sort.csv, _data/ladder2_double_sort_held.csv.
