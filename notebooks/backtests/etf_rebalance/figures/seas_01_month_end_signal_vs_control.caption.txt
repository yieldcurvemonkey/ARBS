The one pre-specified version -- pre-month-end minus post-month-end, averaged WITHIN each month-end cycle so nine overlapping 10-day returns count once -- gives -0.039bp at HAC t -1.31 (p = 0.38) at the traded 10-day horizon. It reaches HAC t -2.14 at 1 day (p = 0.04), which is one of four horizons looked at, and TLH's same contrast has the OPPOSITE sign at the same horizon (+0.019bp, HAC t +2.27). Two funds on the same reconstitution calendar disagreeing on the sign is what a selected effect looks like.
