TLT's par holding of a Treasury that falls below 20 years to maturity and leaves its index. 33 crossings are identified; 23 of them are drawn here, and the 10 that are not are not a data hole - TLT simply owned no position in those bonds to shed, which is what a SAMPLING fund looks like from the other side. Offsets are BUSINESS days off the crossing date; the base is the median par over business days -60 to -21, a level, so no single document sets it. WHAT IT PROVES. The shed is real and it is large: the median position is 5% of its pre-event level 120 business days after the crossing and 1% after 180 - 95% and 99% of the position sold. It is emphatically NOT a month-end cliff - the median is still 82% on the crossing date itself and the selling runs for months afterwards, in tranches. The dashed control settles that this is deletion and not ordinary turnover: the same fund's par in a maturity-matched ~21.5-year bond on the same days is flat. The lower panel is on TLT's denominator on purpose - TLH, the $11bn short fund, buys back only a fraction of what the $47bn long fund sells, so a deleted bond is a net sale to the market rather than an internal transfer. WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE. That the flow moves the price, or that any of it is tradeable. Two identification checks refuse the flow story outright: event amplitude does not scale with TLT's share of the bond's float (corr 0.002, and a tercile sort runs backwards, 0.73bp low-ownership vs 0.43bp high), and reversing the flow direction does not reverse the sign - the same test at the 10-year boundary, where a small seller is replaced by a large BUYER, gives +0.52bp t 2.31 against +0.47bp t 2.93 here. On the trade itself: 1,274 butterflies over 40 entry/exit cells, best gross 0.40bp against a measured 1.09bp cost, 0 of 40 cells net positive even at an optimistic 0.5bp floor. A verified flow is not an edge. NOTE ON CONSTRUCTION. RESULTS.md reports 85-90% shed by day +120 from a prior pass whose offsets are stamped in CALENDAR days over 20 events; on BUSINESS days over these 23 the median is 95% by +120 and 99% by +180. Both describe the same shed - 120 business days is about 172 calendar days - but the axis matters, so it is labelled.
