The strongest seasonal cell anywhere in this study is TLT's August at -0.114bp, HAC t -2.92 against zero over 226 observations. This figure is the two tests that decide whether it is a season.
LEFT, is it carried by one year? 7 of 11 Augusts share the pooled sign, but 2016 alone prints -0.41bp -- more than three times the pooled value -- and 2026 contributes only 3 days. Dropping 2016 takes the cell to -0.080bp and its HAC t against the series' own unconditional mean from -2.25 to -1.48. The placebo bars beside each year are the same August, same maturity strata, holdings permuted away, and they are the same size as most of the real ones.
RIGHT, what did finding it cost? The cell was selected as the largest |HAC t| over 70 calendar cells across four keys. Running the identical search over 100 maturity-matched placebo series gives a null for that maximum: median 2.61, p90 3.13, largest 3.61. The real 2.92 sits at family-wise p = 0.21. It does NOT survive Newey-West once the search is charged, and the dashed line shows it falls further (2.25) once it is measured against the series' own backwards level rather than against zero.
This is RESULTS.md 3.5 happening a second time on a different search: there, a placebo boundary at 28 years beat the real 20-year deletion boundary (2.74 against 2.40); here, a fifth of holdings-free draws beat the best seasonal cell in the study. And the magnitude settles it regardless -- 0.088bp of seasonal deviation against a 0.54bp round trip is 6x too small to trade even if every statistic above had held.
