Rung 0 is the selling edge

Rungs count up from the fund’s deletion boundary, not down from thirty years. Rung 0 is the last three months a bond spends inside the index before the fund must sell it, and it means that on every date and in every fund. That is the one edge the six ladders share, so it is the one they are aligned on.

The pattern is diagonal, and that matters

Streaks run down and to the right: a bond keeps its weight and descends one rung per quarter as it ages. What looks like a queue of dislocations is mostly one sampling policy seen in motion — the same holding, re-labelled each quarter.

It is also why the fund’s position is a poor forecast. An active weight persists for years; the richness it would have to predict has a 17-day half-life.

What sits off the ladder

Rungs exist only where the index carries weight, so a bond the fund still holds after the index has dropped it falls off the axis. That residual is published as off-index above — it runs from 0.4% of TLT on a DV01 basis to 7.7% of TLH by market value, and it is largely the deletion shed in progress.

Index weight sums to 100.0% by construction; fund weight does not, and the gap is that.
